We'll speak up for you

There are a lot of issues that affect young people in Bury, and we want to make sure there being heard. Our main priorities is better youth spaces and activities, safer communities, more jobs and opportunities for 16-18 year olds, and support with the cost of living, but we dont stop there. If a issue affects young people in Bury, we want to hear about it, regardless of what it is.

If you have a concern, we'll do our best to understand it and raise it with the right people. This could be Bury Council, local sports and leisure providers, schools and colleges, transport providers, local businesses or other organisations that can help make a difference. Whether its something big or something that might seem small, we'll listen, speak up on your behalf and do what we can to push for change.

If its affecting young people, it matters to us. Tell us about it, we'll do our best to make sure your heard.

How this works

  1. You tell us what's going on. Happening to you, or just something you think should change. Either's fine.
  2. We listen. Messages, polls, surveys. We look at what keeps coming up.
  3. We decide what to push. The big ones can become campaigns. Other stuff still gets raised with the right people.
  4. We take it to whoever can actually do something. Council, schools, bus companies, businesses. Whoever's responsible.
  5. We come back to you. What they said, and what we're doing next.

If it matters to young people in Bury, we want to hear about it.
